Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

This policy explains how Kuya Ely Tours and Transport Services (“Kuya Ely Tours”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects personal information on kuyaelytours.com. We operate from Sitio Capilis, Suba-Basbas, Lapu-Lapu City, Cebu, Philippines, and follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

01Who we are

We arrange private island tours and van or car rental from Mactan and Cebu. Reach us at info@kuyaelytours.com, +63 920 985 1802, or WhatsApp at the same number.

02Information we collect

  • Account details. Name, email, phone or WhatsApp, and a password you create. Passwords are stored as a secure hash, not as plain text.
  • Google, Facebook, or Apple sign-in. If you use a social button, that provider shares the name, email, and account ID you allow. We do not receive your social password.
  • Bookings and inquiries. Travel dates, destination, guest count, pickup notes, hotel, vehicle choice, and messages you send.
  • Payments. If you pay online, our payment partner processes card or e-wallet details. We receive payment status, not your full card number.
  • Technical data. Cookies or session data needed to keep you logged in, remember a cart, and run the site. We do not sell this data.

03How we use information

  • Create and sign you into a Kuya Ely account.
  • Plan, confirm, or update tours, van rental, and airport transfers.
  • Send booking notices and replies from info@kuyaelytours.com.
  • Process a Book now request or a Pay now checkout.
  • Improve the website and keep it secure.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use Google, Facebook, or Apple sign-in data to advertise on those platforms.

04Sharing

We share information only as needed to run the service: website and email hosting; Google, Facebook, or Apple to finish a sign-in you started; a payment provider when you choose Pay now; drivers or partners on a confirmed trip (pickup and guest details only); and authorities if Philippine law requires it.

05Cookies and accounts

The site uses a session cookie so your cart and login stay on this device until you sign out or the session ends. You can refuse cookies in your browser; some booking features will then ask you to sign in again.

06Retention

We keep account and booking records while you have an account, and as needed to complete a trip or meet accounting and legal duties. You may ask us to close an account. We may keep limited records of a completed booking where the law requires it.

07Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, or withdraw consent for social sign-in, by emailing info@kuyaelytours.com. You may also complain to the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines.

08Children

Accounts are meant for guests 18 years and older. A parent or guardian may include children as trip guests. We do not knowingly create accounts for children.

09Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, and limited staff access. No method of transmission is completely secure. Please use a strong password and keep your login private.

10Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
